Calibration develops shared references and terminology. Agreement and individual repeatability are related but different goals.

## Why it matters

Shared references improve the usefulness of tasting feedback.

## A small experiment

Taste a shared reference and discuss descriptors before comparing scores.

## A misconception to question

> Calibration means every taster must have the same preference.
