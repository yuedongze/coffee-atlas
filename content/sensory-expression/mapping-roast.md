title: "Green + profile → roasted coffee"

A profile acts on a particular green coffee in a particular machine to change its physical and chemical state. Equal time or a similar curve is not proof of equivalent roasted material, even when some green measurements match.

## Why it matters

Separate the process settings from the resulting material.

## A small experiment

Record the lot, initial measurements, actual trajectory, and endpoint for two equal-duration roasts.

## A misconception to question

> Five minutes produces the same roast in every coffee.
