---
title: "Grind consistency"
prerequisites: ["cupping"]
---

Grind preparation affects extraction and the repeatability of tasting. Keep equipment, settings, and sample handling consistent.

## Why it matters

Preparation variance can hide small sensory differences between roasts.

## A small experiment

Prepare duplicate brews using the same grind procedure and compare them blind.

## A misconception to question

> The same dial number means the same grind on every grinder.
