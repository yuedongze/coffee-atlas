Revisit finalists at multiple ages across the intended drinking window, using matched storage and preparation. Compare at equal ages and document when each is preferred; do not assume its earliest ranking will persist.

## Why it matters

The chosen profile must work when people actually drink it.

## A small experiment

Schedule repeat tastings at several points in the expected window and record ranking changes.

## A misconception to question

> One tasting date proves which profile performs best throughout its life.
