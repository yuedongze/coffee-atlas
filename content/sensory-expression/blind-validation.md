---
title: "Blind comparison"
prerequisites: ["evaluate"]
---

Hide profile identities and vary serving order when validating finalists. Include repeat samples and record descriptions before preference; triangle tests can assess distinguishability, not which coffee is better.

## Why it matters

Blinding reduces expectation effects from knowing the roast settings.

## A small experiment

Have another person code finalists and a duplicate baseline, then compare notes before revealing labels.

## A misconception to question

> A triangle-test difference establishes preference or quality.
