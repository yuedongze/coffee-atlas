Texture describes tactile qualities such as perceived weight and smoothness. Separate these observations from aroma and preference.

## Why it matters

Separating tactile observations from flavor makes sensory targets clearer.

## A small experiment

Describe texture at several temperatures without using flavor-note words.

## A misconception to question

> Heavier body automatically means higher quality.
