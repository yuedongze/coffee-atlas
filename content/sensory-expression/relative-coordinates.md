Numbers are a consistent recording convention, not absolute truth. Stable anchors make relative comparisons useful across a session. Keep uncertainty and repeat ratings; changing scale definitions makes apparent distances misleading.

## Why it matters

A sensory vector is meaningful only in a shared measurement context.

## A small experiment

Record A=[2,4,1,3] and B=[3,4,2,2] on four agreed axes, then explain each difference in words.

## A misconception to question

> Any numerical distance between sensory vectors is automatically meaningful.
