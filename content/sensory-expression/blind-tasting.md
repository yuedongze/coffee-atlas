---
title: "Matrix comparison"
prerequisites: ["sensory-language"]
---

Compare a planned array of profiles applied to the same lot, using a fixed brewing protocol and matched rest ages. Record a sensory vector for each candidate across hot, warm, and cool tastings. Look for useful trade-offs rather than a single impressive sip.

## Why it matters

A structured comparison helps navigate the coffee’s response to profile changes.

## A small experiment

Build a table with profile IDs as rows and sensory axes at each tasting stage as columns. Include a repeated baseline.

## A misconception to question

> A single sip or one winning attribute establishes the best profile.
