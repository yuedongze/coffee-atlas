Think in multiple sensory coordinates: acidity, sweetness, floral intensity, fruit intensity, texture, clarity, and unwanted characteristics. Shared descriptors and anchored scales let you compare relative positions, not just collect flavor words.

## Why it matters

A sensory vector describes a cup; the target determines which direction is desirable.

## A small experiment

Rate two coded cups on the same anchored scales and describe their relative differences.

## A misconception to question

> A higher number on every sensory axis always means a better coffee.
