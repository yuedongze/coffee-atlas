Return to each cup several times as it cools. Record the same attributes at each stage and note how clarity, balance, and unwanted characteristics change. Define stages consistently enough to compare the flight.

## Why it matters

A cup is a changing experience rather than one measurement at one sip.

## A small experiment

Make separate hot, warm, and cool entries for every candidate; record temperature or elapsed time.

## A misconception to question

> The first sip adequately represents the whole drinking experience.
