---
title: "Shortlist candidates"
prerequisites: ["evaluate"]
---

Keep several candidates that satisfy different parts of the intended target. Separate promising differences from uncertain ones, and decide what evidence the next comparison needs to supply.

## Why it matters

A shortlist avoids committing too early to a noisy winner.

## A small experiment

Select two or three profiles and explain the trade-off each preserves.

## A misconception to question

> Only the single highest initial rating deserves another test.
