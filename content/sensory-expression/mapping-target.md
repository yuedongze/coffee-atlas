Search for a useful sensory region for this coffee and use case: a balance of acidity, sweetness, aroma, clarity, and acceptable texture with few unwanted characteristics. Different coffees can need different profiles even when some physical attributes look alike.

## Why it matters

The objective is a desired brewed outcome rather than a particular duration or curve shape.

## A small experiment

Write the target sensory ranges and brewing context before selecting a profile.

## A misconception to question

> Matching moisture and density guarantees the same chemical composition and flavor response.
