Represent a cup with several descriptive axes. Rate intensity separately from liking: moderate acidity may fit one target, while higher acidity fits another. Add clarity, bitterness, astringency, or other relevant axes without collapsing them into one score.

## Why it matters

The relative pattern can be more informative than one flavor label.

## A small experiment

Use the same low, medium, and high references to compare two cups on four axes.

## A misconception to question

> The strongest intensity on every axis is always the goal.
