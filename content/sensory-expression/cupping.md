title: "Consistent brewing"
prerequisites: ["cooling"]
---

Choose one repeatable preparation for a comparison: cupping / immersion or the intended customer recipe. Keep water, grind, ratio, rest age, and handling consistent within that comparison. Each method answers a somewhat different question.

## Why it matters

A repeatable brew makes differences between roast candidates easier to interpret.

## A small experiment

Brew duplicate coded samples with one documented method before comparing profiles.

## A misconception to question

> One brewing method reveals every possible expression of a coffee.
