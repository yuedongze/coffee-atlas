The roasted material is then ground and brewed. Water, recipe, equipment, rest age, and tasting temperature shape the sensory result. Roasting affects flavor potential, but a profile is not a portable flavor prescription.

## Why it matters

Judge the combined roast and preparation that the customer will experience.

## A small experiment

Brew one roast with immersion and the intended recipe; note which sensory differences change.

## A misconception to question

> A profile directly specifies flavor regardless of brewing.
