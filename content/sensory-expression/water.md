Water composition and brewing conditions influence extraction and perception. They are part of the measurement setup.

## Why it matters

Brew differences can be mistaken for roast differences.

## A small experiment

Hold water and preparation constant for roast comparisons; investigate brew variables separately.

## A misconception to question

> Every undesirable cup characteristic comes from roasting.
