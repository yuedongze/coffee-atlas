Immersion offers a practical common preparation for comparing samples and returning to them as they cool. Grind and extraction still affect clarity and what you perceive; cupping does not reveal everything or remove brewing effects. Its result may differ from the customer’s recipe.

## Why it matters

It provides a repeatable comparison, with a defined preparation context.

## A small experiment

Use one grind, water, ratio, and tasting schedule for a coded flight. Record sediment or clarity effects.

## A misconception to question

> Cupping is an unfiltered measurement of the bean independent of brewing.
