Shortlist several candidates against your intended sensory target. Compare them blind, repeat the brewing, and revisit them across the intended resting window before choosing which profiles to keep or test next.

## Why it matters

A promising early cup may not be the strongest choice at drinking age.

## A small experiment

Choose two or three candidates and a baseline; schedule coded comparisons through their resting windows.

## A misconception to question

> The highest score immediately after roasting is the final winner.
