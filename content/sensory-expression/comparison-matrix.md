Keep the lot fixed, label every profile, and compare under a common preparation. A table can hold sensory axes, rest age, tasting stage, and uncertainty for each profile. Use repeats and varied tasting order to reduce confounding.

## Why it matters

The matrix connects a known parameter change with an observed response.

## A small experiment

Compare a baseline and nearby profile variants with coded labels and a randomized serving order.

## A misconception to question

> More profiles automatically produce more reliable knowledge.
