---
title: "Customer recipe brewing"
prerequisites: ["cupping"]
---

Prepare coffee as recommended to customers: for example, the same four-pour V60 recipe. This tests the intended experience and can reveal lost clarity or complexity that a cupping preparation did not show. Pouring and technique add variability, so repeat the preparation.

## Why it matters

Customer-like preparation reduces surprises between evaluation and use.

## A small experiment

Document brewer, filter, water, grind, ratio, four pour timings and amounts, and repeat brews.

## A misconception to question

> A good cupping result guarantees a good result with any customer recipe.
