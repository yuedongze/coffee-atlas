title: "Target ranges & trade-offs"
prerequisites: ["sensory-language"]
---

Define acceptable ranges for desirable attributes and limits for unwanted characteristics. Several candidates may meet the target with different balances; preference and customer use determine which trade-offs matter.

## Why it matters

There can be several useful solutions instead of a universal best point.

## A small experiment

Write target ranges for acidity, sweetness, floral and fruit intensity, plus an astringency limit.

## A misconception to question

> A single overall score preserves all relevant sensory trade-offs.
