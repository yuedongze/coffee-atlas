---

The ratio of coffee to brewing water is part of your evaluation method. Record both masses rather than relying on volume estimates.

## Why it matters

A consistent ratio makes the tasting preparation easier to repeat.

## A small experiment

Weigh coffee and water for every sample in a comparison.

## A misconception to question

> Changing the brew ratio cannot affect a roast comparison.
