Changing one parameter at a time is useful for a local comparison. A planned factorial matrix can investigate multiple parameters and their interactions, provided combinations and repeats are designed and recorded deliberately.

## Why it matters

A matrix and a local single-variable test answer different experimental questions.

## A small experiment

Name the parameter being tested, hold other conditions steady, and include a baseline repeat.

## A misconception to question

> A matrix requires changing everything without control.
