Begin with an educated profile based on the coffee, machine, and target. Brew and evaluate, choose a small directional change, and compare it with the baseline. Keep helpful changes, reverse unhelpful ones, and reduce the step near a promising region.

## Why it matters

This is a practical gradient-descent analogy, not a measured mathematical gradient.

## A small experiment

State one sensory gap, predict which parameter change may help, and test a nearby candidate with a repeat baseline.

## A misconception to question

> Each sensory observation points to one unambiguous parameter adjustment.
