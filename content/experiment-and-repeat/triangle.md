A triangle test presents three coded samples, two alike and one different. Interpreting results requires an appropriate test design.

## Why it matters

A structured difference test asks a narrower question than a preference comparison.

## A small experiment

Practice preparing coded samples and randomizing their order before using results to make a decision.

## A misconception to question

> One correct answer proves a reliable difference.
