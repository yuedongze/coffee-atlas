---
title: "Broad profile exploration"
prerequisites: ["experiment"]
---

Test a planned range of profile parameters to map the coffee’s response. This can reveal distinct promising regions, but consumes more coffee and evaluation effort. Use a manageable design and repeated reference batches.

## Why it matters

Broad exploration is useful when you have little confidence in a starting point.

## A small experiment

Choose a small matrix, allocate coffee for repeats, and define which sensory outcomes will guide narrowing.

## A misconception to question

> Trying many unrelated profiles makes a controlled experiment.
