title: "Lot traceability"
prerequisites: ["qc"]
---

Traceability links received coffee, production batches, and finished products. Clear identifiers make investigation possible.

## Why it matters

Batch identifiers make quality investigations practical.

## A small experiment

Trace one finished bag back to its roast batch and green lot.

## A misconception to question

> A product name is a sufficient lot identifier.
