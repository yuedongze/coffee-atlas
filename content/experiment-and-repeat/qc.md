Translate successful candidates from the sample roaster to the production machine, then verify them using the intended brewing and resting conditions. Keep lot traceability, repeatability checks, and sensory release criteria.

## Why it matters

A successful sample roast needs validation at the production scale.

## A small experiment

Compare sample and production batches blind at matched rest ages using the customer recipe.

## A misconception to question

> Copying a sample-roaster curve guarantees an equivalent production cup.
