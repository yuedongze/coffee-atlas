title: "Recheck & change direction"
prerequisites: ["experiment"]

Sensory responses are noisy and parameters can interact. A locally promising profile need not be best elsewhere. Repeat uncertain results, occasionally test another region, and stop when improvements are too small or inconsistent for the intended use.

## Why it matters

Guided search needs checks against noise and local optima.

## A small experiment

Write a stopping rule and one condition that would trigger a wider exploratory test.

## A misconception to question

> Following one direction guarantees the global optimum.
