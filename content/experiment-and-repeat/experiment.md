Two valid strategies are broad profile exploration and guided iteration. A matrix can map a wider range of responses; an informed starting point with small, measured changes can use less coffee. Choose the approach based on uncertainty, resources, and the question.

## Why it matters

Both strategies turn sensory observations into the next planned experiment.

## A small experiment

Choose a search strategy, name the parameters you will vary, and set a tasting and stopping rule.

## A misconception to question

> One search strategy always finds the best profile with the least coffee.
