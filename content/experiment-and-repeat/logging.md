Link lot and profile version to actual roast observations, brewing recipe, rest age, tasting stage, sensory coordinates, and the next decision. Record unsuccessful trials as well as promising ones.

## Why it matters

Linked records connect the process you changed to the cup you evaluated.

## A small experiment

Create one record containing roast ID, brew ID, sensory notes, and your next hypothesis.

## A misconception to question

> A roast curve alone explains the sensory outcome.
