Convection, conduction, and radiation transfer heat to the beans. Their contributions depend on the machine and operating conditions.

## Why it matters

Changes in energy input affect the roast with delays and interactions.

## A small experiment

On a familiar, safely operated machine, compare logged responses to one modest control change.

## A misconception to question

> A burner setting is a direct measurement of heat absorbed by the beans.
