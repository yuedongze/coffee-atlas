Get to know the system that heats, moves, and measures your coffee. Roaster families describe designs, but actual machines often combine several mechanisms.

## Why it matters

Machine context comes before interpreting a roast curve.

## A small experiment

Sketch the heat source, bean movement, air path, and probe locations on your roaster.

## A misconception to question

> A machine category completely predicts its thermal behavior.
