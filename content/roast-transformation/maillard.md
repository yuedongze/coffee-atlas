The browning interval, often described from yellowing to first crack, is a useful observational phase. Explore the chemistry below, remembering that these transformations overlap other phases and have no universal probe-temperature boundaries.

## Why it matters

Browning is meaningful, but phase duration alone cannot describe the chemistry.

## A small experiment

Compare two controlled roasts and record color and sensory differences without assuming one reaction explains everything.

## A misconception to question

> A longer browning phase always creates more sweetness.
