---
title: "Surface to center"
prerequisites: ["heat-transfer"]
---

Heat flows inward along temperature gradients. The center responds with a delay: elapsed time matters alongside the surface-temperature history and the bean’s changing thermal properties.

## Why it matters

You control the surroundings more directly than the temperature at the center.

## A small experiment

Compare two temperature histories in a heat-transfer model and note why equal total time need not give equal center temperature.

## A misconception to question

> The center temperature is a function of time alone.
