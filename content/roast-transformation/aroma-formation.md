title: "Aroma formation & loss"
prerequisites: ["maillard"]
---

Roasting both creates and loses volatile aroma compounds. The balance evolves across overlapping reactions and changing conditions, rather than a list of flavors appearing at exact timestamps.

## Why it matters

An aroma category is a clue, not a unique chemical diagnosis.

## A small experiment

Track broad aroma changes during roasting and compare them with coded cup notes.

## A misconception to question

> One aroma note identifies one reaction at one exact stage.
