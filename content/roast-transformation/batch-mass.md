Batch mass changes the load on the machine and can affect airflow, agitation, and probe exposure.

## Why it matters

A stable batch mass removes an avoidable source of variation.

## A small experiment

Record exact green batch mass for each repeat roast.

## A misconception to question

> Half the batch mass always needs half the energy setting.
