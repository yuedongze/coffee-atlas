Conductivity, heat capacity, density, moisture, and pore structure influence internal heating. These properties change during roasting; green bulk density alone does not specify thermal diffusivity.

## Why it matters

A single green measurement cannot predict the whole internal temperature field.

## A small experiment

Record bean size, moisture, and density separately when comparing roast responses.

## A misconception to question

> Denser green coffee always needs one fixed heat adjustment.
