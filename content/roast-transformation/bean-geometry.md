Size and shape affect the distance heat must travel. In a simplified diffusion model, a characteristic heating time scales with distance squared divided by thermal diffusivity; real beans also lose moisture and change structure.

## Why it matters

Size can matter even when the external heating looks similar.

## A small experiment

Compare thickness and shape across lots before attributing a response only to origin or variety.

## A misconception to question

> All beans of the same mass have the same internal heating behavior.
