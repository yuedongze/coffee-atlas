Drum machines tumble beans inside a rotating chamber. Their thermal behavior varies with design and operating conditions.

## Why it matters

Knowing the machine’s design helps interpret its controls and response.

## A small experiment

Identify the heat source, airflow path, and agitation controls on a drum machine.

## A misconception to question

> All drum roasters transfer heat in the same proportions.
