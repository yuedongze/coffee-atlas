Build a lot-specific record of when your coffee tastes best for its intended brewing method. Coffee with Dongze field notes can document early cups to avoid, preferred windows, and later decline; personal results will be added from actual tastings.

## Why it matters

A useful window comes from repeated observations under stated conditions.

## A small experiment

Record roast ID, packaging, storage, rest age, recipe, and cup notes at several intervals. Mark the preferred window and uncertainty.

## A misconception to question

> One universal rest window applies to every coffee and brew method.
