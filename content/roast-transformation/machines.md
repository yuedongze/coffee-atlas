Drum, fluid-bed, and other roasters differ in heat storage, airflow, agitation, and control response.

## Why it matters

Controls and temperature readings do not transfer directly between machines.

## A small experiment

Map your machine’s controls and review its operating and fire-safety instructions before roasting.

## A misconception to question

> Matching displayed temperatures matches the same process on different machines.
