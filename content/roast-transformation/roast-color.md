title: "Roast color"

Color is an endpoint observation. Whole-bean and ground measurements describe different aspects and require consistent preparation.

## Why it matters

A consistent color measurement gives you another way to compare endpoints.

## A small experiment

Measure repeated samples with the same preparation and instrument.

## A misconception to question

> Equal surface color guarantees an identical cup.
