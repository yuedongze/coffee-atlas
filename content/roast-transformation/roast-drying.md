Early roasting heats the bean and removes moisture. Yellowing is a practical visual landmark, not proof that all water has left the bean.

## Why it matters

This provides the first reference point in a simple roast overview.

## A small experiment

Record charge, yellowing, and the uncertainty in observing color change.

## A misconception to question

> Water removal stops exactly at yellowing.
