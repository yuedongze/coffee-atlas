---
title: "Parameterized template"
prerequisites: ["baseline"]
---

Define a reusable profile template with named inputs: batch mass, initial machine state, energy and airflow steps, event references, and endpoint criteria. Version the template and record actual values.

## Why it matters

Explicit parameters turn a one-off roast into a reproducible experiment.

## A small experiment

Create a baseline template and a copy that changes one named parameter.

## A misconception to question

> Copying a curve alone captures every condition needed to repeat a roast.
