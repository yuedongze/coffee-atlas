---
title: "Convection"
prerequisites: ["heat-transfer"]
---

Convection exchanges heat between moving gas and bean surfaces. Gas temperature and flow conditions both matter.

## Why it matters

Air movement is part of the thermal process, not just exhaust management.

## A small experiment

Trace how heated air reaches the beans and exits your roaster.

## A misconception to question

> Air temperature alone describes convective heat transfer.
