Raising external heat can increase the surface-to-center temperature difference. It does not instantly heat the center, and may change the surface much faster. Interpret internal heating through both time and boundary conditions.

## Why it matters

More external power is not an independent control for the bean’s center.

## A small experiment

Design a comparison that records total time, endpoint, and sensory results rather than only power settings.

## A misconception to question

> Extra power instantly eliminates the internal heating delay.
