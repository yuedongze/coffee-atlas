Describe the intended cup and brewing context before adjusting the roast. A target should allow meaningful comparison.

## Why it matters

A pleasing curve is not itself a sensory objective.

## A small experiment

Write three acceptance criteria for your next roast: desired expression, brew context, and an unwanted characteristic.

## A misconception to question

> There is one universally best roast for a coffee.
