Energy input and airflow influence heat transfer and the removal of moisture and smoke. Their effects depend on the roaster.

## Why it matters

The same airflow adjustment can behave differently across machines.

## A small experiment

Record airflow settings alongside energy settings in your baseline; change only one factor in a planned comparison.

## A misconception to question

> More airflow always speeds up or always slows down roasting.
