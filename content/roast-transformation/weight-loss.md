title: "Weight loss"
prerequisites: ["endpoint"]
---

Mass loss compares green and roasted batch mass. Consistent weighing and handling make comparisons more meaningful.

## Why it matters

Mass loss provides a simple batch-level observation alongside color and tasting.

## A small experiment

Calculate (green mass minus roasted mass) divided by green mass for three batches.

## A misconception to question

> Weight loss alone measures flavor development.
