---
title: "Chlorogenic acids (CGAs)"
prerequisites: ["maillard"]
---

Chlorogenic acids change and generally decline with increasing roast exposure, forming other compounds along the way. These changes do not occur at one universal phase boundary or probe temperature.

## Why it matters

Chemical measurements and sensory impressions answer different questions.

## A small experiment

Compare published measurements with their specific roast conditions before applying them to your machine.

## A misconception to question

> A particular displayed temperature removes all CGAs.
