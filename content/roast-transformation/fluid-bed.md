Fluid-bed machines use moving air to support and agitate coffee. Air movement and heat delivery are closely connected.

## Why it matters

Understanding airflow’s role helps explain how the system responds to adjustments.

## A small experiment

Sketch the air and bean paths for a fluid-bed design.

## A misconception to question

> An airflow setting translates directly between drum and fluid-bed machines.
