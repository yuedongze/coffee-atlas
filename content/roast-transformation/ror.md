prerequisites: ["sensors","first-crack"]
---

Rate of rise estimates how quickly measured temperature changes. Sampling, smoothing, and probe behavior affect the curve.

## Why it matters

It helps describe momentum when interpreted alongside other observations.

## A small experiment

Compare the raw temperature trace with two smoothing settings. Notice which apparent features change.

## A misconception to question

> Every crash or flick proves a sensory defect.
