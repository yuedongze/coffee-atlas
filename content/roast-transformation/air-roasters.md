Air roaster is a broad term for machines that rely strongly on heated air. Fluid-bed roasters are one overlapping family; the labels are not mutually exclusive.

## Why it matters

Design details are more useful than treating labels as rigid categories.

## A small experiment

Compare how two air-based machines move beans and control heat.

## A misconception to question

> Air roasters and fluid-bed roasters are always separate categories.
