Begin with an intended cup, batch size, and repeatable machine state. Turn controls and endpoints into named parameters, then build a matrix of recorded profiles and sensory outcomes. Repetition and controlled comparisons make the data interpretable.

## Why it matters

Repeatability makes a later change easier to interpret.

## A small experiment

Repeat an established safe roast twice and compare event timings, weight loss, and blind cup notes.

## A misconception to question

> Optimization should come before repeatability.
