title: "Oxidation & aroma change"
prerequisites: ["cooling"]

Exposure to oxygen contributes to changes in roasted coffee during storage. Packaging, temperature, and handling affect the outcome; resting includes competing changes rather than endless improvement.

## Why it matters

Degassing and staling should not be treated as the same process.

## A small experiment

Compare matched portions stored under documented conditions and taste them blind.

## A misconception to question

> More rest always improves coffee.
