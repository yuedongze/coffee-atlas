Sugars are transformed during roasting through several reaction pathways. Their products contribute to aroma and other compounds; perceived sweetness is not a direct reading of residual sugar.

## Why it matters

Sweetness in the cup cannot be prescribed by one phase duration.

## A small experiment

Compare perceived sweetness in coded samples at a consistent brew strength.

## A misconception to question

> A longer Maillard phase always means a sweeter cup.
