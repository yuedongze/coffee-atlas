Reducing sugars and amino compounds participate in Maillard reactions that contribute aroma compounds and brown melanoidins. Other reactions overlap; the named Maillard phase is an observation window, not a chemical on/off switch.

## Why it matters

A phase label cannot isolate one reaction or guarantee a flavor.

## A small experiment

Compare color and cup notes across repeat roasts without attributing a difference to one reaction alone.

## A misconception to question

> Maillard reactions occur only between yellowing and first crack.
