title: "Organic acids"
prerequisites: ["maillard"]
---

Different acids can form or break down as roasting proceeds. Their balance depends on the starting material and roast history; acidity in the cup is also shaped by extraction and perception.

## Why it matters

Acidity is not a single compound that disappears on a timer.

## A small experiment

Record acidity quality and intensity separately across a controlled roast comparison.

## A misconception to question

> All acids decline at the same rate throughout roasting.
