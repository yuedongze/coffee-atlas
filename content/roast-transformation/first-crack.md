First crack is an audible physical event associated with structural changes and pressure within the beans. Its onset can be gradual.

## Why it matters

It is a useful reference event when observed consistently.

## A small experiment

Use a consistent onset definition across three roasts and note how confidently you identified it.

## A misconception to question

> First crack occurs at one universal displayed temperature.
