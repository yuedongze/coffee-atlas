A probe samples a small part of the roasting system and responds with its own thermal lag. Position, airflow, contact, and instrument settings shape the reading. We use these observations to test hypotheses about the whole system; they do not directly reveal every bean or its center.

## Why it matters

Treating readings as observations prevents false precision.

## A small experiment

Compare event timing and temperature on repeat roasts before drawing conclusions from small differences.

## A misconception to question

> Displayed bean temperature is the uniform temperature of every bean.
