Roast color, mass loss, time, and sensory results describe different aspects of the outcome. No single measure captures development.

## Why it matters

A consistent endpoint helps isolate differences between experiments.

## A small experiment

Measure whole-bean color and mass loss consistently, then compare them with blind cup notes.

## A misconception to question

> Development-time ratio alone defines roast quality.
