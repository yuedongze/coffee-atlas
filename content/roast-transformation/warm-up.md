A machine’s thermal state affects its response. A repeatable warm-up routine is part of a repeatable baseline.

## Why it matters

Repeatable initial conditions make roast comparisons easier to interpret.

## A small experiment

Document your manufacturer-approved warm-up and between-batch routine.

## A misconception to question

> One displayed temperature completely describes machine heat storage.
