The interval from the observed onset of first crack to the endpoint is commonly called development. Reactions and structural changes continue, but development of the coffee is not confined to this interval.

## Why it matters

Time after crack is useful context alongside the full trajectory and endpoint.

## A small experiment

Compare time after first crack, total time, color, and cup notes together.

## A misconception to question

> Development-time ratio alone measures how well the center is roasted.
