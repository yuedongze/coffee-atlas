Follow a simple overview from drying and yellowing through browning, first crack, and development. These are useful landmarks in a continuous process: moisture loss and chemical reactions overlap.

## Why it matters

Events give you reference points for describing a trajectory.

## A small experiment

Annotate yellowing, first crack, and drop on a logged roast; note uncertainty in each event.

## A misconception to question

> The start of first crack is the start of all flavor development.
