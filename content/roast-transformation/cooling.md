After roasting, gas release and storage-related changes affect brewing and sensory expression. Explore CO₂, oxidation, and a resting window established for each coffee and brewing method.

## Why it matters

Evaluation timing can change your interpretation of a roast.

## A small experiment

Evaluate the same roast at several documented resting intervals using consistent brewing.

## A misconception to question

> Every coffee has the same ideal rest period.
