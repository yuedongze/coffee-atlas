---
title: "CO₂ & degassing"
prerequisites: ["cooling"]
---

Roasting generates gases, including CO₂, some of which remain in the bean and escape during storage, grinding, and brewing. Release rates vary with roast and storage conditions.

## Why it matters

Gas release changes brewing behavior and is one part of resting.

## A small experiment

Compare the same roast at recorded rest ages with a consistent brewing method.

## A misconception to question

> All coffees finish degassing after the same number of days.
