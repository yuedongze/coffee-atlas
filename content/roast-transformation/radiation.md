---
title: "Radiation"
prerequisites: ["heat-transfer"]
---

Hot surfaces emit thermal radiation. The amount exchanged depends on temperatures, surfaces, and geometry.

## Why it matters

Recognizing multiple heat-transfer mechanisms improves your model of the machine.

## A small experiment

Identify which hot surfaces face the bean bed.

## A misconception to question

> Radiation requires direct physical contact.
