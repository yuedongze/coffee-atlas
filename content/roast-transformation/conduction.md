---
title: "Conduction"
prerequisites: ["heat-transfer"]
---

Conduction transfers heat through contact. Its contribution depends on surfaces, contact conditions, and temperature differences.

## Why it matters

Contact is one of the mechanisms behind energy transfer to the coffee.

## A small experiment

Identify where beans contact heated surfaces in your machine.

## A misconception to question

> Contact heating is the only heat source in a drum roaster.
