A sample roast supports evaluation of a lot. Consistent preparation helps distinguish green differences from roasting variation.

## Why it matters

Repeatable sample preparation makes buying comparisons more dependable.

## A small experiment

Compare duplicate samples roasted and cupped with the same procedure.

## A misconception to question

> Sample roasting removes all influence of roasting on the cup.
