Harvest selection influences the material entering processing. Ripeness, sorting, and lot separation deserve their own observations.

## Why it matters

Harvest observations help explain variation in the material before roasting.

## A small experiment

Ask a supplier how ripeness was assessed and how cherries were sorted.

## A misconception to question

> A harvest date tells you whether every cherry was ripe.
