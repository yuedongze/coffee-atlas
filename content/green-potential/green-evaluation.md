Bring together traceability, physical observations, and a sample roast to form an initial picture of the coffee.

## Why it matters

A documented starting point makes later roast decisions more interpretable.

## A small experiment

Create a one-page lot record with physical measurements, sample cup notes, and an initial target.

## A misconception to question

> Green measurements replace sensory evaluation.
