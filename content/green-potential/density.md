Bulk density includes space between beans. Bean dimensions and size distribution describe different physical properties.

## Why it matters

Consistent measurements help characterize and compare lots.

## A small experiment

Repeat a bulk-density measurement with a consistent filling method. Note screen size separately.

## A misconception to question

> Bulk density and individual-bean density are interchangeable measurements.
