Honey and pulped-natural processes retain some mucilage during drying. Names and color categories vary between producers.

## Why it matters

Producer-specific details prevent misleading comparisons based on labels alone.

## A small experiment

Ask what a honey label means in the context of a particular farm.

## A misconception to question

> Honey processing means honey was added to the coffee.
