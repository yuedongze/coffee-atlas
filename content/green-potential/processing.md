Washed, natural, and honey describe broad processing families. Fermentation and drying choices vary considerably within them.

## Why it matters

Processing helps explain differences you may encounter in the cup.

## A small experiment

Taste a washed and a natural coffee, noting other differences that prevent a controlled comparison.

## A misconception to question

> A processing label tells you the full fermentation history.
