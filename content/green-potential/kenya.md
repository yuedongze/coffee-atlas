Use Kenyan lot records to connect growing location, producer information, varieties, and processing with what you observe in the cup. Keep the roast and brewing context in your notes.

## Why it matters

Controlled comparisons help separate origin expectations from observations.

## A small experiment

Compare two Kenyan samples with a consistent tasting preparation; record the lot differences before interpreting the cup.

## A misconception to question

> Country of origin alone predicts a coffee’s acidity.
