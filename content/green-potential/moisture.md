title: "Moisture content"
prerequisites: ["physical-attributes"]
---

Moisture content is the fraction of a sample’s mass attributed to water. Measurement depends on the instrument and method.

## Why it matters

Water affects storage behavior and the energy involved in roasting.

## A small experiment

Measure repeated samples from one lot using the same instrument and preparation. Record the spread.

## A misconception to question

> One moisture number tells you the correct charge temperature.
