title: "Physical defects"
prerequisites: ["moisture"]
---

Visible defects can indicate damaged or irregular material. Use a consistent grading method and document the sample size.

## Why it matters

Consistent records help compare incoming lots and investigate quality issues.

## A small experiment

Photograph and label visible defects from a green sample; compare with a recognized grading reference.

## A misconception to question

> Every visible irregularity has the same sensory consequence.
