---
title: "Physical attributes"
prerequisites: ["processing"]
---

Characterize green coffee with moisture content, water activity, density, and visible defects. These are different observations of the material, not interchangeable quality scores.

## Why it matters

A consistent physical record helps compare lots and interpret later roasting observations.

## A small experiment

Create a lot sheet with separate entries for moisture, water activity, density, and physical defects.

## A misconception to question

> One physical measurement determines the right roast profile.
