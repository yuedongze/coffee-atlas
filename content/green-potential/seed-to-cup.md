Coffee is an agricultural seed whose history shapes what roasting can express. Species, growing conditions, processing, and storage all contribute.

## Why it matters

A roast starts with a material, not an empty canvas.

## A small experiment

Trace one bag back through its origin, variety, processing, harvest, and storage. Record what is unknown.

## A misconception to question

> Roasting can create any desired flavor in any coffee.
