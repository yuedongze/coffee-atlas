Washed processing removes fruit material before the main drying stage. Producers use different methods to remove mucilage.

## Why it matters

Understanding the process helps you interpret the supplier’s lot description.

## A small experiment

Describe the sequence of fruit removal, fermentation, washing, and drying for one lot.

## A misconception to question

> Every washed coffee follows an identical process.
