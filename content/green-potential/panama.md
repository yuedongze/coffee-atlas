Investigate a Panamanian lot at the farm and variety level. Separate the coffee’s documented identity from reputation, price, and your expectations.

## Why it matters

Blind observation helps limit reputation and price bias.

## A small experiment

Taste a coded Panamanian sample before reading its marketing description, then compare your observations.

## A misconception to question

> A prestigious origin guarantees a particular flavor or quality.
