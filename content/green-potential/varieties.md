Species and varieties differ genetically. Their expression also depends on the environment and how the coffee is handled.

## Why it matters

Genetics provides context for the green material and its possible expression.

## A small experiment

Build a side-by-side record of variety, processing, and cup notes for three lots.

## A misconception to question

> A variety label guarantees a particular flavor.
