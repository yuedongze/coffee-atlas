Explore Ethiopian lots through their producer or washing-station information, region, variety descriptions, and processing records. Treat each lot as a specific example rather than a national flavor template.

## Why it matters

Lot-level detail helps turn an origin label into useful roasting context.

## A small experiment

Compare two Ethiopian lot sheets and identify which details are documented and which are missing.

## A misconception to question

> Every Ethiopian coffee should taste alike.
