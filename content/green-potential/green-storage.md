Packaging, time, and storage conditions affect green coffee. Keep lot history and observations alongside inventory records.

## Why it matters

Lot history helps you notice changes rather than attributing every difference to the roast.

## A small experiment

Record packaging, arrival date, and storage conditions for one lot.

## A misconception to question

> Green coffee remains unchanged in storage.
