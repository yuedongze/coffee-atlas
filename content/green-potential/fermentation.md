Fermentation involves microbial activity during processing. The sequence, duration, and conditions provide more useful context than a label alone.

## Why it matters

A process timeline separates documented handling from assumptions about flavor.

## A small experiment

Record the fermentation steps reported for one lot, distinguishing known conditions from assumptions.

## A misconception to question

> A fermentation label is a complete production record.
