Compare Colombian lots using their regional, producer, variety, harvest, and processing information. A country label is a starting point for learning about the material.

## Why it matters

Comparing lots reveals variation hidden by a country label.

## A small experiment

Choose two Colombian lots and build a comparison of their documented growing and processing details.

## A misconception to question

> All coffees from one country call for the same roast profile.
