---
title: "Drying"
prerequisites: ["processing"]
---

Drying removes moisture after harvesting and processing. The drying method, handling, and recorded conditions help describe how the green coffee was prepared.

## Why it matters

Drying records add context that a fermentation label cannot provide.

## A small experiment

Document how one lot was dried and which conditions the producer monitored.

## A misconception to question

> Drying and fermentation describe the same process.
