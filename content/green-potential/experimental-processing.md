---

Experimental processing is a broad label for approaches beyond a producer’s usual practice. Ask for the actual sequence, conditions, and any introduced materials rather than inferring a method from the name.

## Why it matters

Clear processing records make unfamiliar labels more interpretable.

## A small experiment

Request a processing timeline for one experimental lot and distinguish documented steps from sensory claims.

## A misconception to question

> Experimental processing describes one standardized method.
